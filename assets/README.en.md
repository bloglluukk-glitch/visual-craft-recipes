# AI-generated reference images

[Korean](README.md) | **English**

The five PNGs introduced in v0.2 were newly generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. They depict fictional unbranded products and explanatory illustrations; client originals and external photographs were not copied. SVG source files are not provided.

| File | Role | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| bottle_reference.png | Reference for bottle shape, cap, label layout without lettering, and glass material | Not a real product photograph or an optical measurement |
| metal_reference.png | Reference for the shape and material of a satin-silver cylinder | Not a real metal photograph or surface measurement |
| glass_lighting.png | Illustration of outlines against light and dark backgrounds | Not a baseline/recipe comparison under identical settings |
| metal_lighting.png | Illustration of a reflection band, dark edge, and consistent surface color | Not a reproduction or measurement of actual rendering settings |
| motion_plan.png | Plan for partial product rotation and camera approach | Not actual video frames or verified motion |

[GENERATION_RECORD.json](GENERATION_RECORD.json) records the prompts, reference relationships, dimensions, and file hashes. The tool was the built-in image generator. Model identifiers, seeds, and generation settings were not exposed by the tool, so they were not inferred. The saved prompts do not guarantee reproduction of identical pixels.

Attach the product PNGs as image references. Although they look photographic, they are all fictional AI-generated products. Use them to guide shape, label placement, and material, while specifying background, lighting, and motion changes separately in the recipe. For a real product, substitute a photo you have permission to use and record the source and permission.

Teaching illustrations help communicate the intended screen appearance. Fine proportions or reflections may differ from the input; do not treat them as edits that have passed pixel-identity checks. The circular arrow in the motion illustration indicates partial rotation, not a full 360-degree spin.

The geometric diagrams and SVGs from v0.1 are not included in this public edition.
