# Share results and improvements

[Korean](CONTRIBUTING.md) | **English**

When a recipe succeeds or fails on a new model, leave a record that others can follow under the same conditions. Submit an [Issue](https://github.com/bloglluukk-glitch/visual-craft-recipes/issues) or [Pull Request](https://github.com/bloglluukk-glitch/visual-craft-recipes/pulls).

1. Include the recipe ID, tool, model, execution date, input method, output size, and whether seeds are supported.
2. Attach the input image and the prompts you used. Use assets you created or have permission to share.
3. Attach results from both the baseline and modified conditions. Do not select only a successful image to make a performance claim.
4. Separate observation from interpretation. "The cap changed shape in two of three runs" is an observation. "Reference strength may have been too low" is an interpretation.
5. Copy and fill in [experiment_record.json](templates/experiment_record.json). Check that the record does not expose personal or account information.
6. If you changed wording, explain which single condition you changed. When multiple comparison conditions change, label the result as exploratory.

Start a new recipe with [recipe_template.en.md](templates/recipe_template.en.md). Without observations, keep its status experimental and do not claim it has been validated.

You must have permission to share contributed documentation, prompts, images, and templates under this project's [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.en.md). Identify any other sources and their terms of use. Do not attach personal information, secret keys, or client materials you do not have permission to share.
