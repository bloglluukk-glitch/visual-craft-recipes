## How this collection was made

Lighting, materials, camera movement, and prompt composition topics were selected from the Visual Craft knowledge of creator bloglluukk-glitch. Codex helped rewrite the material for sharing and prepare experiment inputs and review checklists. The built-in image generation tool created the fictional products and explanatory illustrations.

The underlying knowledge was organized with AI assistance in production and review. Familiar optical and photographic principles are not claimed as the creator's exclusive invention. The attached images are fictional AI-generated products and illustrations, not results from recipe comparisons under the same conditions. Actual image generation prompts and reference relationships are recorded in [GENERATION_RECORD.json](assets/GENERATION_RECORD.json).

The contribution is a way to divide the problem, prioritize preservation, prepare comparison inputs, and decide what to change after a failure.

## Distinguish explanation from verification

- **Background principles:** general photography and material concepts explained by the official or manufacturer sources above.
- **Editorial judgment:** priorities and repair order proposed by this project.
- **Experiment design:** aspect ratios, four-second duration, partial rotation amount, and repeat count. These choices start a comparison; they are not verified optimal values.
- **Recipe comparison observations:** none yet. Five reference images have been generated, but baseline/recipe comparisons are separate experiments. Anticipated failures are review targets, not cases observed in this collection.

Technical support currently concerns concepts. Entire external documents have not been exhaustively rechecked against every current version.
